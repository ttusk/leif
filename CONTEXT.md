# Leif

Leif organizes a concurso study plan and records the learner's progress through it.

## Language

**Concurso**:
The exam or selection process whose study plan Leif organizes.
_Avoid_: Contest

**Matéria**:
A major area of knowledge in a Concurso and one step in its study cycle.
_Avoid_: Subject, discipline

**Assunto**:
An atomic syllabus topic studied within a Matéria.
_Avoid_: Topic, item

**Recurso**:
A progress-bearing study source belonging to a Matéria, such as a PDF, video, course module, book, link, or question notebook. It may cover zero, one, or several Assuntos and does not require a numeric completion target.
_Avoid_: Study item, item, material

**Acesso**:
A URL or vault file through which the learner reaches a Recurso. An Acesso is not independently ordered or progress-bearing.
_Avoid_: Resource reference, material

**Meta do recurso**:
An optional measurable completion target for a Recurso, expressed in pages, questions, lessons, or minutes. A Recurso without a Meta do recurso is completed explicitly by the learner.

**Registro de estudo**:
An independent, dated historical fact belonging to one Concurso and exactly one Matéria, optionally narrowed to one Recurso and one Assunto.
_Avoid_: Session, event, sitting

**Estado do ciclo**:
The learner-controlled Matéria and Recurso recommendation position in a Concurso study cycle. Recording study never changes it implicitly.
_Avoid_: Contest state

**Mural**:
The free-form reference area of a Concurso, including official links and planning notes.
_Avoid_: Wall
